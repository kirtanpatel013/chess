Rules

This app plays standard chess with no clock — take as long as you like. It enforces:

  • Check — if your king is under attack, the king square is highlighted and you must make a move that gets it out of check.
  • Checkmate — if a player is in check with no legal move to escape it, the game ends immediately and that player loses.
  • Castling — king and rook can castle (kingside or queenside) if neither has moved, the squares between them are empty, and the king isn't in, through, or landing on check. Press Ctrl + F1 to auto-castle if legal.
  • En passant — if an opponent's pawn advances two squares and lands beside yours, you may capture it as if it had only moved one square, but only on your very next move.
  • Pawn promotion — when a pawn reaches the far rank, choose Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight; it never auto-promotes to Queen.
  • Stalemate — if the player to move isn't in check but has no legal move at all, the game ends in a draw.
  • Threefold repetition — if the exact same position occurs three times, the game ends in a draw.
  • Insufficient material — if neither side has enough pieces left to possibly checkmate, the game ends in a draw.
  • Legal / illegal moves — only moves that follow standard chess rules and don't leave your own king in check are allowed; anything else is rejected with an "Illegal Move" message.
  • Resign — either player can resign at any time; the other player wins.
  • Draw offer — either player can offer a draw, which the other player accepts or declines.

Not included on purpose: no AI/computer opponent, no timer or move clock, and this is local pass-and-play on one device — not online multiplayer.
